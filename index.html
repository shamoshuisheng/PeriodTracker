Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Lunra Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase.

Is my data private?

Yes. All cycle data is stored locally on your device. No third-party analytics, no tracking. iCloud sync (Premium) uses your private iCloud account.

How does the cycle prediction work?

Lunra learns from your historical period data to predict your next period, ovulation day, and fertile window. The more you log, the more accurate it becomes.

Does Lunra work on iPad?

Yes. Lunra fully supports iPad with a sidebar navigation layout.

How do I export my data?

Upgrade to Premium, then go to Settings → Data → Export CSV or Export PDF.
